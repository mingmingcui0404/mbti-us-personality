How to Use Your Result

Personality typing is most useful when it gives you better questions, cleaner language, and a kinder way to notice your own habits.

Work

Use your type to understand focus, conflict, planning, feedback, and the kind of work environment that brings out your best effort.

Relationships

Notice how you ask for support, share feelings, solve disagreements, and show care when your energy is low.

Growth

Your type is not a fixed script. It is a map of tendencies you can strengthen, balance, and sometimes happily ignore.
